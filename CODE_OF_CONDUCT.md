# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or other
protected characteristic.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Being respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, sexualized content or attention, threats, doxxing, and sustained
disruption of project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies in project spaces and when representing the project
elsewhere. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently exclude participants whose behavior violates this
Code.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through the contact
options on the [Argonaut790 GitHub profile](https://github.com/Argonaut790).
Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
Anyone reviewing a report must disclose conflicts of interest and recuse
themselves when appropriate.
